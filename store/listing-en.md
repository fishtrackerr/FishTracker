## Full description

```
Fish Tracker is an offline-first fishing register for anglers who want their logbook on the phone—even at the water with weak signal.

Track fishing sessions and catches, save lakes and spots, attach photos, and pull weather when you are online. Everything stays on your device. There is no account and no cloud database.

Features
• Active session tracking with rods, spots, and bites
• Catch log with photos, measurements, and notes
• Lakes and spots you return to
• Gallery of trip and catch photos
• Statistics for your local history
• Optional weather and geocoding when online
• Optional AI assistant if you add your own API key
• Backup and restore via JSON export/import
• Optional PIN lock for shoulder-surfing protection

Privacy
Your fishing data stays on the device unless you export a backup or use optional online features (weather, maps links, or AI). See the in-app privacy policy for details.
```
